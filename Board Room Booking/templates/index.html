Centralized meeting scheduling & coordination.

Welcome!

The Meeting Management System (MMS) is the centralized platform for planning, scheduling, coordinating, and managing meetings conducted at the Corporate Office. The system is designed to enhance collaboration, optimize the utilization of conference facilities, reduce unnecessary travel by branch Principals and staff, and ensure that meetings are conducted in a well-planned and efficient manner.

Through this platform, stakeholders can conveniently access meeting schedules, conference room availability, meeting agendas, organizers, and participant details, enabling better planning and minimizing operational disruptions.
